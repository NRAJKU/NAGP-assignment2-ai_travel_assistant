# Visit Singapore — 24 Hours in Singapore
source_url: https://www.visitsingapore.com/content/visitsingapore/en/travel-tips/travelling-to-singapore/itineraries/24-hours-in-singapore/
source_type: official itinerary

## Itinerary patterns
The official itinerary presents a compact way to experience several Singapore highlights in a short period. It includes heritage and cultural areas, Marina Bay and Gardens by the Bay, food experiences, and evening options around the Singapore River/Clarke Quay area.

## Planning use
Use this source for stable examples of how attractions can be grouped into a practical day. The application should treat the grouping as an itinerary idea, not as a promise about current opening hours or live conditions.
