# Visit Singapore — Singapore Itineraries
source_url: https://www.visitsingapore.com/singapore-itineraries/
source_type: official itinerary collection

## Planning guidance
The official itinerary collection provides ideas for different visitor profiles and trip durations, including family-oriented experiences, food, culture, shopping and multi-day sightseeing.

## Family planning
For families, itineraries can combine attractions with breaks and shorter travel segments. Indoor attractions can be used as alternatives when outdoor conditions are uncomfortable.

## Cultural planning
Heritage districts and museums can be grouped into cultural-focused days, while food experiences can be added around the same neighbourhoods to reduce unnecessary travel.
