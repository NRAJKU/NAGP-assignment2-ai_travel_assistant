## Districts and neighbourhoods
The guide describes Singapore through areas such as Downtown Core, Chinatown, Little India, Kampong Glam and other districts. These areas provide different combinations of heritage, food, shopping and sightseeing.

## Transportation
Singapore has an extensive MRT/LRT and public bus network. Public transport is a practical default for many tourist movements. Walking can also be useful when attractions are clustered in the same district.

## Practical use
Use neighbourhood information to create geographically coherent itineraries. For live service changes, do not rely on this static guide; use a current service or explicitly tell the user that the knowledge base does not provide live status.
