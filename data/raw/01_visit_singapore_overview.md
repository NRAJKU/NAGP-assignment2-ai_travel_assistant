# Visit Singapore — Official Tourism Guide
source_url: https://www.visitsingapore.com/
source_type: official tourism website

## Scope
This source is the official Singapore tourism portal. It is used for stable destination knowledge such as attractions, neighbourhoods, food and local experiences, travel tips and visitor planning.

## Knowledge notes
Singapore combines modern city districts with heritage areas such as Chinatown, Little India and Kampong Gelam. Marina Bay, Gardens by the Bay, Sentosa and major cultural attractions are common sightseeing options.

Public transport is an important way for visitors to move around the city, with MRT and buses providing broad coverage. Visitors should check the current operating details when a question depends on a live disruption or schedule.

The portal also contains practical visitor guidance and destination ideas for different interests and trip lengths.
